## 📝 Summary  

We learned about how to represent words by dense vectors with two architectures: continuous bag of words and skip-gram. We compared the result of these representations to the previous RNNLMs and NNLMs based on semantic and syntactic features. Finally, we read the conclusion part of the paper and discussed about it.
